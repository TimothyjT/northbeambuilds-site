Common questions

Where do the answers come from?

Your own website and the info you give us — your hours, prices, services, and service area. If the answer isn’t there, the box says so and takes a message instead of guessing.

What happens when it can’t answer?

It asks for a name and number. Every lead lands in your private lead view right away; an optional push to a webhook you control, or email via your own mail relay, can be arranged at setup.

Does it work on my kind of website?

Almost always. If your site can carry a small chat box, we can set it up. We’ll tell you before you pay if yours can’t.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. $49 once. That’s it.
