What it does

Three steps, start to finish

  1. 1

    You tell us about your business on one short call — services, hours, prices, service area.

  2. 2

    We build the website and the booking calendar. You see it and approve it before it goes live.

  3. 3

    Customers pick a time and book it themselves. You get a notice for every booking — no phone tag.

A sample moment — shown with made-up names

Who it's for

A good fit — and when it's not

This is for you if…

  • Your shop has no website yet.
  • Your site is old, slow, or has no way to book.
  • You lose jobs because booking means calling during work hours.

This is not for you if…

  • Your shop already has a modern website with booking that works.
  • Your business mainly needs a big online store. That's a different, bigger build — ask us and we'll tell you straight.
One thing to know: This is the website itself, with booking built in. The chat tools — the FAQ Agent and the AI Chatbot — answer questions on a website. They can be added to a site like this later.
