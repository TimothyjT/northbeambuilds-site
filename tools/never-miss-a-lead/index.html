What it does

  1. A call, form, or message comes in and no one can answer.

  2. Your customer gets a reply in under a minute — a text back that says you got it and asks what they need.

  3. Every lead lands in one inbox, urgent ones flagged first, so nothing gets lost.

Sample moment

Sample: Saturday, 6:40 PM. A homeowner's AC dies. The call rings out. Under a minute later her phone buzzes: “Sorry we missed you — reply with what's happening and we'll get you on the board.” She replies. The lead lands in the owner's inbox flagged “Emergency — AC out.”

Why speed matters

Conversion was 8× higher when the first call came within 5 minutes (XANT, 2021 — 5.7 million leads studied).

In an audit of 2,241 companies, the average reply to a web lead took 42 hours — and 23% never replied at all (Harvard Business Review, 2011).

These studies are from 2011–2021 and most measured phone calls, not texts. They are about one thing: the first shop to respond usually wins the conversation.

Who it's for — and who it's not

It's for you if…

  • Your shop misses calls while you're out on jobs.
  • Your shop is closed evenings and weekends, but customers still call.
  • You find leads scattered across voicemail, texts, and website forms.

It's not for you if…

  • You have a full office team that already answers every call and form right away.

Never-Miss-a-Lead follows up after a missed call or message, by text. It does not answer the phone live. If you want a voice to pick up the call itself, see the AI Phone Receptionist.

Questions

How fast is “instant”?

Under a minute, day or night.

What do I have to do?

A short setup form — about five minutes. You fill it in yourself — nothing is pre-filled; many answers are quick picks from a list. It collects your hours, services, and where leads should go. We build it, you test it on your own phone, then it goes live. One timing note: text replies need a carrier registration (A2P) that takes 2–5 business days. The email path goes live right away, and texting turns on as soon as the carriers finish.

What if the customer replies with a hard question?

The reply starts the conversation and captures the details. Anything real gets flagged to you right away — you take it from there.

Can I cancel?

Yes. It's month-to-month. Cancel anytime and you owe nothing further.
