AI Phone Receptionist — for shop owners who can’t pick up while they’re working: it’s a voice that answers your calls, answers basic questions, and books the appointment or takes a message, so callers reach help instead of voicemail.

A sample moment — shown with made-up names

Sample: The owner is under a house fixing a pipe. The phone rings twice and the receptionist picks up. The caller asks about a leaking water heater; it books Thursday at 9 AM. The owner gets the booking and a short summary — and never stopped working.

Who it’s for — and who it isn’t

This is for you if…

  • You miss calls all day because you’re out on jobs.
  • After-hours callers reach your voicemail and never call back.
  • You run a one- or two-person shop with no one free to answer the phone.

This is not for you if…

  • You already have a full-time receptionist answering every call.
  • You need emergency dispatch calls handled with no human involved. Real emergencies and hard calls always go to a person.

Never-Miss-a-Lead texts back after a call is missed. This one answers the call live, by voice. Many shops start with the text-back, then add this.

Common questions

What if a call is complicated, or an emergency?

It goes straight to you. You set the rules on day one: which calls the voice handles, and which ones ring a human, every time.

Can it book into my calendar?

Yes. It books from your real open times — and you'll hear it do exactly that on your test call, before you pay anything. You get the booking and a short summary of every call.

Does it answer at night and on weekends?

Yes, whenever you set it to answer. Many owners use it only when they can’t pick up; it can cover the hours you choose.

Can I hear it first?

That’s the deal. A live test call happens before any payment.
