Terms of Service

The deal, in plain words. These terms cover everything Northbeam Builds sells on this site. When you pay for one of our tools, this is the agreement between you and us. Last updated October 15, 2026.

Who we are

Northbeam Builds is a small software studio in Austin, Texas. We build and run the tools listed on our pricing page. Questions about anything on this page: email northbeambuilds@gmail.com and a person will reply within one business day. If you need our mailing address, ask by email and we'll send it.

What you're buying

Each product is described in full on its own page. This is the short version, with the full price:

  • Never-Miss-a-Lead — $199 setup + $99/month. One intake channel — missed calls, website form fills, or after-hours messages, whichever you choose at setup — with a reply in under a minute to every lead on it, one lead inbox, urgent-lead flags, and a monthly report.
  • Website FAQ & Lead Capture Agent — $49, one-time. A chat box on your website that answers visitor questions from your own pages and captures the lead when a visitor wants to be contacted.
  • Website + Online Booking — from $249, one-time. A multi-page website (home, services, about, contact) with an online booking calendar and booking notices sent to you. A full online store is a bigger build and is quoted separately.
  • AI Chatbot — $149 setup + $49/month. A chat assistant on your site that answers customer questions around the clock from content you approve, and takes the visitor's details when it can't answer.
  • AI Phone Receptionist — $399 setup + $199/month. An AI voice answers your phone, answers questions from your approved content, and books appointments into your calendar. Your plan includes 300 call minutes a month; past that, extra minutes are $0.50 each, itemized on your invoice, and we alert you at 240 minutes so a busy month never surprises you. A minute is the full call, answer to hang-up, including hold and transfer time.

Not included in any product: logo design, photography, writing your service descriptions from nothing, or moving a large existing website's content over. If what you need is bigger than the box, we tell you before we start and quote it as custom work. We never bill extra work without your agreement first.

When it goes live

The setup form takes about five minutes. You fill it in yourself — nothing is pre-filled. Many answers are quick picks from a list; the rest are short answers about your business. When your clock starts depends on the product:

  • Website FAQ & Lead Capture Agent: live 1 business day after payment, content, site access, and the hosting in place are all in — whichever comes last starts the clock.
  • AI Chatbot: live within 24–48 hours after payment, your content, and site access are all in — whichever comes last starts the clock.
  • Never-Miss-a-Lead: email follow-up live within 48 hours after payment, your completed intake, and access to your lead source are all in — whichever comes last starts the clock.
  • Website + Online Booking: live within 5 days after payment, your content, and domain and site access are all in — whichever comes last starts the clock.
  • AI Phone Receptionist: live in 3–7 days after payment, your intake information, and phone and calendar access are all in — whichever comes last starts the clock, and only after you approve a test call.

Website FAQ & Lead Capture Agent — read this before you pay: this agent runs on hosting we are still standing up, so it cannot go live yet and the go-live clock above has not started. Once the hosting is in place, the clock runs exactly as stated in its line above.

One delay sits outside our hands. Before a business number can send texts, the phone carriers require a registration (called A2P), and that registration typically takes 2–5 business days. Everything else goes live within the window above, and the texting turns on as soon as the carriers finish. We'll tell you where it stands either way.

The guarantee

If we miss the live window for your product, your setup fee comes back in full. Email us, ask, and we refund it the same day we read the message. No forms, no argument.

If you pay and never finish the setup form, we refund everything automatically on day 14. You don't have to ask, and we won't hold money for a product we never built.

If your product is live but something in it is wrong (a price we typed incorrectly, an answer that doesn't match your services), that's a fix, not a refund. Tell us and we correct it. Fixes like that are part of the monthly tuning described below.

Free trials

Some products offer a free trial, stated on the product's page: what it includes, how long it runs, and the one price to continue. No trial takes a card and no trial charges anything — when it ends, it simply stops. Continuing is a separate purchase at the price on that page.

Partners and referrals

If you refer a client to us under the partner terms on our Partners page, you get 15% of everything that client pays us in their first year. Setup fees and monthly payments both count. Shares are paid monthly, and a payment that is refunded comes off the amount your share is worked out from.

Instead of a referral, you can choose white-label delivery for a client: we deliver under your name, and you set your own price to the client. You pick one option per client, referral or white-label, in writing, before work starts.

Paying, and the monthly plan

Payments run through Stripe. Stripe takes your card; we never see or store your full card number.

Setup fees are one-time. Monthly plans start the day you buy and renew on the same date each month. Every monthly plan is month-to-month. There is no long-term contract.

Cancel anytime: use the subscription link in any Stripe receipt, or email us and we'll cancel it for you. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you've already paid for, and we don't refund part of a month that's already running. After you cancel, your lead and conversation logs stay available to export for 30 days.

If a monthly payment fails, Stripe retries the card on its own. If it still hasn't gone through, your tool keeps running for 7 more days, then pauses. Pausing never deletes your data, and paying the open invoice turns it back on.

Your content and your data

Your tools answer only from content you approve: the services, prices, and hours from your setup form. When a question falls outside that content, the tool takes the visitor's details instead of guessing.

We don't train models on your business data. We don't sell it or hand it to advertisers. Your conversation and lead logs belong to you, and you can open them in your admin at any time.

What we need from you

Accurate setup information. We build exactly what the form says, so check the services and prices before you send it. If you approved something that's wrong, the fix is free under tuning; just tell us.

Access where the product needs it: your website login so we can install the chat box, your phone setup for the receptionist, your calendar for bookings. We use that access only to build and run your product.

Support and monthly tuning

Email is the support channel: northbeambuilds@gmail.com. A person replies within one business day.

Your monthly plan covers monitoring, a short monthly report on what your tool did, and up to two small changes a month: a price update, a new answer, a schedule change. Bigger work is quoted first, as an add-on or a custom job.

Limits

We're a software service, not your employee, and we can't promise a specific number of leads, bookings, or revenue. Numbers you see in a demo come from published industry benchmarks. They show how the math can work, not a result we guarantee.

If something we did causes a loss, our total liability is capped at the fees you paid us in the three months before it happened.

Changes

If we change a price for customers already paying it, we email at least 30 days before the change takes effect, and it never applies to a month you've already paid for. If we change these terms in a way that matters, we'll post the new version here and email current customers.

That's the whole deal. If any part of it is unclear, email northbeambuilds@gmail.com before you buy and we'll explain it in plain words.
