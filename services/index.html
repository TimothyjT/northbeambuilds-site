What we do

Four kinds of work, all for local service businesses. Every job is scoped and priced in writing before it starts, and monthly services are month-to-month.

01

Websites & Booking

Who it’s for: shops with an old website, no website, or a site that can’t take a booking — where the phone is still the only way to get a job.

What’s included: a clear, fast website built around how customers actually choose a shop — your services in plain words, your prices where you want them public, a contact form that reaches you right away, and online booking so customers can pick a time without calling. Written for phones first, since that’s where most customers find you. When it’s done, you get the site, the logins, and a short handover note — it’s yours.

What it costs: website + booking builds start at $249, one-time. The exact price depends on page count and whether you need online payments, and you get it in writing before work starts.

Useful add-on: the Website FAQ & Lead Capture Agent ($49 flat) answers visitor questions on the new site from your own prices and hours.

02

Never Miss a Lead

Who it’s for: shops that lose jobs to silence — calls that ring out on a job, website forms answered the next morning, inquiries that land after closing and wait until Monday.

What’s included: pick the channel that costs you more jobs to start — missed calls or website forms. Every one gets a reply in under a minute, in your business’s name. The customer’s details and the whole conversation land on your phone, so you take over when you’re free. If a message ever fails to send, you’re told. Once a month you get a one-page report: how many people reached out, how fast each was answered, and what came of it.

What it costs: $199 to set up, then $99 a month. First month total: $298. Month-to-month.

Delivered by: Never-Miss-a-LeadProduct details →

Why speed matters: in a 2021 study of 5.7 million leads, conversion was 8× higher when the first call came within 5 minutes (XANT, 2021). An audit of 2,241 companies found the average reply to a web lead took 42 hours — and 23% never replied at all (Harvard Business Review, 2011). Read both honestly: they’re older studies of sales leads, not Austin home services, and most measured phone calls. Every one of them points the same way: the shop that answers first does better.

04

Reviews & Appointments That Hold

Who it’s for: shops whose reviews sit unanswered because the work comes first, and appointment businesses that feel it when a booked customer never shows.

Review Autopilot

$149 flat

An audit of your own public reviews. Every unhappy reviewer (1–3 stars) is sorted for recovery, and you get private recovery drafts and public reply drafts — you read, edit, and send them yourself; nothing goes out in your name without your say-so. It also sets up a request flow that asks every customer for a review the same way. Nothing is ever offered in exchange for removing or changing a review.

Product details →

No-Show Guard

$99 setup + $29/month

Booking with a card deposit, taken through your own Stripe payment link — the money goes to your account, not ours. A confirmation email goes out at booking and a reminder email two days before. If a customer never pays the deposit, the slot is held, not confirmed, and opens back up when the hold runs out.

Product details →

What it costs: Review Autopilot is $149 once; an optional monthly loop afterwards is $29 a month per location, agreed separately. No-Show Guard is $99 to set up, then $29 a month starting when it goes live on your bookings. Month-to-month.
