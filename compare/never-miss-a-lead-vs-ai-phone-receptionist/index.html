A text back after the miss, or a voice that answers the call?

Both exist for the same moment: a customer calls and you cannot pick up. Never-Miss-a-Lead lets the call ring out, then texts the caller back in under a minute so the conversation starts anyway — $199 to set up, then $99 a month. The AI Phone Receptionist picks the call up instead: a voice answers, handles the basic questions, and books the job or takes a message — $399 to set up, then $199 a month. Pick by what your callers need after they dial: a fast reply they can text back to, or a person-shaped answer on the call itself.

Two things to know before you pick

1. The AI Phone Receptionist cannot be bought today, and its test call is not available this week: our phone platform account is still in approval, and we will arrange your test call as soon as the account clears. You can hear sample calls now on the receptionist site. If you need cover for missed calls on an agreed date right now, the text-back is the one that can start.

2. Never-Miss-a-Lead's text replies are not on from the minute you pay. Texting needs a carrier registration (A2P) that takes 2–5 business days; the email path goes live right away, and texting turns on as soon as the carriers finish. The free trial covers the form-and-email path only, not texts.

Which one fits

Pick Never-Miss-a-Lead if:

  • Your missed callers mostly need a fast reply and a way to explain the job — a leak, a dead AC, a door that won't close. A text they can answer from the driveway does that.
  • You would rather keep the monthly bill at $99 and do the booking yourself once the lead is in your inbox.
  • Your problem is wider than the phone: website forms and after-hours messages also sit unanswered until morning.

Pick the AI Phone Receptionist if:

  • Your callers expect to talk: older customers, urgent jobs, people who hang up on voicemail and will not text back.
  • Booking on the call is the point — the appointment should be in your calendar, at a real open time, before the caller hangs up.
  • You run a one- or two-person shop and there is simply no one free to answer during the day.

What both do, either way:

  • Cover the calls you can't take, day or night, on the hours and channels you choose at setup.
  • Hand anything hard straight to you instead of guessing at it.
  • Run month-to-month. Cancel any month and you owe nothing further.
  • Carry the same guarantee: live by the date we agree, or the setup fee comes back in full.

Questions and answers

What is the difference between Never-Miss-a-Lead and the AI Phone Receptionist?

Never-Miss-a-Lead follows up by text after a call is missed. The Phone Receptionist answers the call live, by voice.

What does each one cost in the first month, and in a year?

Never-Miss-a-Lead is $298 in the first month ($199 setup + $99), then $99 a month — $1,387 if you keep it a full year. The AI Phone Receptionist is $598 in the first month ($399 setup + $199), then $199 a month — $2,787 over a full year, which is $1,400 more. Both are month-to-month, so you can cancel any month and owe nothing further. The receptionist's price includes 300 call minutes a month; extra minutes are $0.50 each, and we alert you at 240 minutes. The text-back has no per-message bill.

What happens when a call is complicated, or an emergency?

It reaches you either way. With Never-Miss-a-Lead, the text starts the conversation and anything real is flagged to you right away — you take it from there. With the AI Phone Receptionist, you set the rules on day one: which calls the voice handles, and which ones ring a human every time. Real emergencies always go to a person, and the receptionist does not dispatch crews.

Can I try one before I pay?

Yes, both, and neither asks for a card. Never-Miss-a-Lead has a 14-day free trial on the website-form path — every form fill gets an instant reply by email, and the trial stops on its own at day 14; text replies are not part of the trial. The AI Phone Receptionist starts with a live test call on your own phone, with your business name and prices, before any payment. One honest limit: our phone platform account is still in approval, so the test call is arranged as soon as the account clears. Sample calls are on the receptionist site now.

How fast does each one reach the customer?

The receptionist reaches them first, because it answers the call itself instead of letting it ring out. Never-Miss-a-Lead reaches them in under a minute after the miss — a text that says you got it and asks what they need, so the conversation starts before they dial the next shop.
