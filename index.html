The problem, plainly

Most lost jobs aren't lost on price. They're lost on silence.

The missed call

You're under a house, on a roof, or driving between jobs. The phone rings, nobody can answer, and there's no second chance — the caller moves down the list.

The overnight form

A website inquiry sent at 9 pm gets answered at 9 am — if it's noticed at all. By then the customer has usually already booked someone who replied first.

The follow-up gap

Even a good first reply dies without a nudge. One unanswered text and the conversation — and the job — quietly ends. Consistency is the hard part, not effort.

Common questions

Asked often, answered straight

Which intake flow should I start with?
Whichever is losing you more work. If most leads call and you're often unable to answer, start with missed-call text-back. If your website form regularly sits unanswered overnight, start there. You can add the second flow later — quoted in writing first.
Does this replace my phone system or website?
No. The system works alongside what you already have — it watches for the missed call or the new form submission and responds. Nothing about how your customers reach you has to change.
How does the $99 starter deal work?
The starter deal is a separate offer for a different product: the first three clients get the Website FAQ & Lead Capture Agent — an assistant on your website that answers common questions and captures visitor details — for $99 instead of the regular $149. It doesn't change the Never-Miss-a-Lead System, which stays at its standard $497 setup + $297/month.
What does the $297/month actually cover?
Hosting and messaging costs, monitoring (including the failure alerts), upkeep of your flow, and your one-page monthly report. It's the full running cost of the system — there is no separate per-message bill from us.
How fast can it be live?
Within days of the agreement in most cases. Your agreement names a specific live date, and if we miss it, the setup fee is refunded — that's written into the guarantee above.
What if I want to cancel?
Email us and it's cancelled at the end of the current month. Month-to-month means exactly that — no notice period games, no exit fee, no contract to buy out.
