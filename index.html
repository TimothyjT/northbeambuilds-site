Common questions

Asked often, answered straight

Should I start with missed calls or website forms?
Whichever is costing you more jobs. If customers mostly call and you're often too busy to answer, start with missed-call text-back. If your website form regularly sits unanswered overnight, start there. You can add the other one later — priced in writing first.
Does this replace my phone system or website?
No. The system works alongside what you already have — it watches for the missed call or the new form submission and responds. Nothing about how your customers reach you has to change.
What does Never-Miss-a-Lead cost in total?
$199 to set it up, then $99 a month. Your first month is $298 total, and every month after that is $99. There are no other fees from us — no per-message bill, no exit fee.
How does the $49 FAQ Agent work?
It's a separate, smaller offer: the Website FAQ & Lead Capture Agent — a chat box on your website that answers common questions from your own prices, hours, and services, and takes a name and number when it can't answer. It's $49 flat right now (regular price $149), with no monthly fee. It doesn't change the Never-Miss-a-Lead System, which is $199 setup + $99/month.
What does the $99/month actually cover?
Hosting and messaging costs, monitoring (including the failure alerts), upkeep of your flow, and your one-page monthly report. It's the full running cost of the system — there is no separate per-message bill from us.
What happens when it can't answer a question?
It doesn't guess. It takes the customer's name and number, tells them you'll follow up, and sends the whole conversation to your phone. You stay in control of anything it isn't sure about.
Who owns my website and my customer details?
You do. Your website, your content, and your customer details stay yours. If you cancel, the system switches off — nothing of yours is held hostage.
How fast can it be live?
Within days of the agreement in most cases. Your agreement names a specific live date, and if we miss it, the setup fee is refunded — that's written into the guarantee above.
What if I want to cancel?
Email us and it's cancelled at the end of the current month. Month-to-month means exactly that — no notice period games, no exit fee, no contract to buy out.
