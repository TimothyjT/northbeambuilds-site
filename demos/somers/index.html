1. The call isn't lost

Instead of ringing out to voicemail after hours, the missed call triggers an immediate text. The homeowner knows they were heard — and doesn't call the next plumber on the list.

2. The details are captured in the thread

Name, address, and problem come back in one conversation, so the owner wakes up to a usable lead — not a missed-call notification with no context.

3. Emergencies are flagged, not buried

Words like burst, leak, and flooding flag the lead as “Emergency — active leak” in the owner inbox below. Try it yourself in the form — the same logic runs there.
